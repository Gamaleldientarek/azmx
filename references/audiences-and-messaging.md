# AZMX Audiences and Messaging

Who AZM X speaks to, and the exact line each audience gets. Read this before writing any copy: a headline, an email, a post, a proposal, a deck. Source: the AZM X Unified Communication Strategy.

**Core messages are verbatim.** They are the messaging, not prose to restyle. Quote them as written, or write a tighter line that carries the same claim. Never soften the claim, never swap the brand it belongs to.

## The five brands

AZM X is the parent house: a leading Saudi digital consultancy. Its philosophy line is **"Designing the Future of Experience."** Four product and community brands sit under it.

| Brand | What it is | Motions |
|---|---|---|
| **AZM X** | The consultancy. Strategy, design, delivery, evidence. | B2G · B2B · Internal |
| **Colab** | UX research platform. The evidence engine. | B2G · B2B · B2C |
| **Majarah** | The region's UX gathering and community hub. | B2C |
| **Clix** | ERP for SMEs. Invoicing, finance, operations. | B2B |
| **Anatomi** | Design benchmark and pattern library for Saudi digital products. | B2G · B2B · B2C |

Check the matrix before you pick a voice. It gives each brand's **primary** motion, so use it to choose the lead voice: a B2G brief leads with AZM X, with Colab and Anatomi as the evidence layer underneath, and all internal communication is AZM X.

**The matrix is not a gate.** It is a compression of several deck slides rather than a slide of its own, and two slides this skill reproduces cut across it. The deck tags Sales & Demo Presentations `B2G · B2B` with Colab, Majarah, Clix, and Anatomi as the brands, reproduced faithfully in `communication-strategy.md`; and the week-4 Decision-Maker email for Majarah is tagged `B2G · B2B · B2C` with a Conversion CTA. Majarah has a real B2B and B2G face — sponsorship, partnership, executive attendance, employer brand — and that is the commercial reason a consultancy runs a community brand at all. Clix likewise appears in B2G and B2B sales and demo contexts. Lead with the primary motion the matrix names, then check the channel plan before ruling a brand out of a brief.

## Internal audiences

Three segments. All brands, all AZM X-issued.

| Segment | What they need to know | Core message to them |
|---|---|---|
| **Leadership** | Business performance at a glance, strategic alignment across departments, and the risks and opportunities on the horizon. | *"Your vision is being executed effectively. We are aligned on our strategic priorities and are proactively managing performance to drive results"* |
| **Team Leads** | Cross-functional priorities, resource allocation, and the specific goals and metrics their teams own. | *"You have the clarity, context, and support needed to lead your team to success. We are empowering you to make decisions that align with our shared goals"* |
| **All Employees** | Company direction, how their role feeds collective results, and news about culture and shared wins. | *"Your work is valuable and directly contributes to our shared success. You are a crucial part of a winning team, and we are succeeding together"* |

## The eight personas

### B2G — government leaders focused on mission delivery, compliance, and public trust

| Persona | Brands | Pain points | Core message to them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Directors / Heads** | AZM X | Need a proven, low-risk path to modernisation, and a partner who knows public-sector complexity well enough to deliver cost-effectively without disrupting the core mission. | *"We provide secure, compliant, and cost-effective solutions to modernize your public services"* |
| **Leads** | Colab · Anatomi | Need proof that modern, agile practice is achievable with the right tools, and to see how research and benchmarking platforms change what their teams can build. | *"Empower your agency with the tools to build accessible and trustworthy digital government services"* |

### B2B — the leaders who approve budgets and buy for their teams

| Persona | Brands | Pain points | Core message to them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C-Suite** | AZM X | Need a partner who de-risks major projects, holds budget and schedule, and produces the competitive edge the business needs to lead. | *"AZM X is the strategic partner that guarantees delivery and drives innovation"* |
| **Product / UX Heads** | Colab · Anatomi | Need a better way to run their team's workflow: fewer bottlenecks, enforced consistency, higher-quality shipping. | *"Equip your team with the integrated tools to ship better products, faster"* |
| **Operations** | Clix | Need one source of truth for core business processes: automation for inefficient workflows, visibility, and enough control to scale. | *"Clix provides the central platform to automate operations and control your business"* |

### B2C — the hands-on individuals who use the products daily

| Persona | Brands | Pain points | Core message to them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Researchers** | Colab · Anatomi | Need one tool that covers planning through presenting, and makes qualitative analysis faster. | *"Colab is the all-in-one toolkit that streamlines your research from plan to presentation"* |
| **Designers** | Anatomi | Need a reliable source of high-quality inspiration when stuck: a curated library of proven, real-world patterns. | *"Anatomi is your personal library of elite designs to inspire creativity and accelerate your workflow"* |
| **Industry Professionals** | Majarah | Need a central hub for the region's creative and tech community, where they can meet peers, learn from experts, and grow a network. | *"Majarah is the hub for the creative and tech community to connect, learn, and be inspired"* |

## Audience tags

Every channel plan tags each item with the audience it serves. Use the same tags when briefing, so a piece can be traced to a persona.

| Tag | Means |
|---|---|
| `B2G` | Government. Directors/Heads, Leads. |
| `B2B` | Private-sector buyers. C-Suite, Product/UX Heads, Operations. |
| `B2C` | Individual practitioners. Researchers, Designers, Industry Professionals. |
| `Internal` | Leadership, Team Leads, All Employees. |
| `All` | Every external motion. Rare; use only when the piece genuinely serves all three. |
| `N/A` | Operational task with no audience-facing output, such as a technical SEO audit. |

A piece tagged for two motions still leads with one. Two tags describe reach, not two headlines.

## Language

Internal communication is Arabic-first: the nineteen internal initiatives are all Arabic-run, and eighteen of the nineteen are Arabic-named. The exception is **Great place to work**, named in English after the GPTW certification (see `editorial-calendar.md`). External communication is typically English, with Arabic for market-facing work, emails, and logo lockups. When a core message is delivered in Arabic, carry the claim, not the sentence structure, and follow the translation guidance in `content-prompts.md`.

## How to use a core message

1. Identify the motion first, then the persona. The persona message is sharper than the motion message; prefer it when you know who is reading.
2. Quote the core message verbatim in strategy documents, briefs, and internal alignment material.
3. In market-facing copy, treat it as the brief. Keep the claim, cut the padding, obey `voice-and-tone.md`. The core messages carry deck vocabulary AZMX house voice bans — *empower*, *elevate*. Do not carry that vocabulary into a headline.
4. One message per surface. A page that serves B2G and B2B leads with one and supports with the other.
5. Never mix persona messages across brands. Anatomi does not promise what Colab promises.
